Native macOS app

Your Reminders. Your Macs. Your Cloudflare.

Task Ferry connects Apple Reminders across Macs without moving your tasks into somebody else's cloud.

One app. Two roles.

Bridge Mac

Reads and updates Apple Reminders locally. Reminders stays the source of truth.

Remote Mac

Connects securely to your bridge and gives you the same native Task Ferry experience.

Bring your own Cloudflare

Your account. Your resources. Your control.

You authorize Task Ferry with narrowly scoped Cloudflare permissions. It creates a Tunnel, DNS record, Access application, and service token inside your Cloudflare account, then revokes its temporary setup access.

Task Ferry does not run a relay, host an account for you, or keep a copy of your reminders. Your bridge remains bound to localhost and Cloudflare carries the encrypted connection between your Macs.

Built for private data

  • Apple Reminders remains canonical. Task Ferry fetches reminders from EventKit when you use it.
  • Credentials stay in Keychain. Tokens are not written to logs or ordinary files.
  • Two layers of authentication. Cloudflare Access protects the route; Task Ferry protects the bridge API.
  • No Cloudflare CLI required. Setup and the tunnel connector are built into the app.
