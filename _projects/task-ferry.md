Task Ferry is a free, open-source Mac app that ferries Apple Reminders between a personal Mac and a work Mac. A bridge runs on the personal machine and talks to Reminders through EventKit. A client on the work machine shows Today, Tomorrow, and your lists over a Cloudflare Tunnel.

There's no account, no hosted database, and no server of mine in the middle. Apple Reminders stays the source of truth.
