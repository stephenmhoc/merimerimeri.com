Write in Markdown, tap Publish. It connects to Ghost and Micro.blog, keeps unsent writing in drafts, and gets out of the way. Titles are optional.

It's built for how I write, so it isn't on the App Store. Grab the Mac app below, or join the TestFlight for iPhone and iPad.
