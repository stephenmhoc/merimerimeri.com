Quill is a free, open-source Markdown editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Write in Markdown, tap Publish. It connects to Ghost and Micro.blog, keeps unsent writing in drafts, and gets out of the way. Titles are optional.

It's built for how I write, so it isn't on the App Store. Grab the Mac app below, or join the TestFlight for iPhone and iPad.
